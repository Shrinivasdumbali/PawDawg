About PawDawg

Our mission is to create a better world for street dogs.

Our Mission

At PawDawg, we believe every street dog deserves a safe and loving environment. Our mission is to provide solutions for the challenges faced by street dogs, including lack of shelter, food, and medical care. By connecting communities, we aim to foster a world where these animals can thrive.

Our Vision

Our vision is to see a society where every street dog is given the care they deserve. We strive to create awareness about the issues faced by stray animals and mobilize efforts to ensure no dog is left behind.

What We Do

  • Facilitate adoptions for newborn puppies.
  • Provide a platform for reporting emergency rescues of injured or harmed animals.
  • Collect small donations to fund shelter and food facilities for street dogs.
  • Raise awareness about the importance of animal welfare in our communities.
